這一章跟你的關係：你問過「為什麼是 evaluating it 不是 to evaluate it」。答案就在這一章：一句話只能有一個真正的動詞，其他動作要「變裝」才能進句子。to V、V-ing、V-ed 就是三套衣服。
3-1

一句話只有一個動詞

第二個動作進句子前，要先換衣服。

英文一句話只有一個「當家的動詞」，它帶時態。其他動作想擠進來，得改成 to V、V-ing 或 V-ed，這三種叫動狀詞——還是動作，但不當家了。

We need to confirm the quantity.　need 當家，confirm 穿 to 進來。

We need confirm the quantity. ← 兩個動詞打架

We need to confirm the quantity.

POINT
  • 一句只有一個當家動詞；第二個動作要穿 to、-ing 或 -ed。
  • to = 朝向未來；-ing = 進行中／事情本身；-ed = 被動、完成。
3-2

to V：箭頭指向還沒發生的事

to 就是第 1 章那個「朝向」的箭頭。

to the factory 是朝向工廠；to confirm 是朝向「確認」這個動作——還沒做，接下來要做。所以 want、plan、need、decide、hope、agree 這些「指向未來」的動詞，後面都接 to V。

to V 當什麼例句
受詞（③ 的 O）We plan to visit the supplier next month.
They agreed to extend the deadline.
目的（為了…）I called to confirm the schedule.
We made a trial batch to check the process.
形容前面的名詞We have three issues to discuss.
There's nothing to worry about.
形容詞後面It's hard to say.　We're ready to ship.　I'm happy to help.

「for 人 to V」：誰去做

It's difficult for us to meet the deadline.　We need the customer to approve the drawing first.

to V 前面放一個人，就是指定那個動作由誰做。

三個常用的 to V 公式

too … to V：The sample is too big to fit.（太大裝不進去）
… enough to V：The cable is strong enough to pass the pull test.
in order to V：We changed the material in order to reduce cost.（正式一點的「為了」）

POINT
  • to V = 指向還沒做的事：want / plan / need / decide / hope / agree + to V。
  • 「為了」直接用 to V：I called to confirm.
  • for 人 to V 指定做的人；too…to、enough to 是現成公式。
3-3

V-ing：把動作變成一件「事」

-ing 把動作變成名詞，像一張照片。

testing 不是「正在測」，是「測試這件事」。所以能當主詞、受詞、介係詞後面的東西。finish、enjoy、keep、avoid、consider、suggest、mind 後面接 V-ing，因為它們面對的是「這件事本身」，不是未來。

to V：朝向未來的箭頭

We plan to test it.

還沒測，打算去測

V-ing：事情本身的照片

We finished testing it.

測試這件事，做完了

V-ing 出現的地方例句
主詞Changing the material will take two weeks.
受詞（固定接 V-ing 的動詞）We finished testing.　Please avoid using this part.
We're considering switching suppliers.　Would you mind sending it again?
介係詞後面（一定是 -ing）Thank you for sending the file.　We're interested in buying more.
before shipping　after receiving the PO　by reducing the thickness

介係詞後面永遠 -ing，這是你最常漏的

Thank you for send the drawing. / Thank you for to send…

Thank you for sending the drawing.

We look forward to receiving your PO. ← 這個 to 是介係詞，所以後面 -ing

同一個動詞，接 to 和接 -ing 意思不同

to V（朝向未來）V-ing（已經是事實）
stopWe stopped to check the line.
停下來去檢查		We stopped checking the line.
不再檢查了
rememberRemember to send the report.
記得要去寄（還沒寄）		I remember sending the report.
記得寄過（已寄）
tryWe tried to reduce the cost.
努力嘗試		Try using a thinner wire.
試試看這個方法
POINT
  • V-ing = 動作變成一件事：finish / avoid / consider / mind / keep + V-ing。
  • 介係詞後面只能 -ing：Thank you for sending、look forward to receiving。
  • stop / remember / try：to 看向未來，-ing 看已經發生的事。
3-4

V-ed：被做了的東西

-ed 貼在名詞上，表示它「被處理過」。

a tested sample = 被測過的樣品。a damaged connector = 被弄壞的連接器。相對地，-ing 貼在名詞上是「正在做／會做這個動作的」：a running line = 運轉中的產線。

-ing：主動、正在

the operating engineer
a growing market

-ed：被動、完成

the tested sample
a damaged connector

情緒形容詞：-ing 是來源，-ed 是感受

The delay is frustrating.（延遲讓人煩，來源）　We are frustrated.（我們很煩，感受）

The result is surprising.　I was surprised.

The training was boring.　I was bored.

I'm interesting in this project. → I'm interested in this project.

放在名詞後面的長版本

the samples tested last week　the drawing attached to this email　the parts made in Vietnam

一個字放名詞前面，一串字放名詞後面。

Please see the file attached. / Please find attached the revised quotation.

POINT
  • -ing 修飾名詞 = 主動、正在；-ed = 被動、已完成。
  • 情緒：事情 -ing（surprising），人 -ed（surprised）。interested，不是 interesting。
  • 一個字放名詞前，一串放名詞後：the file attached to this email。
3-5

分詞構句：把兩句壓成一句

兩句主詞一樣，前一句可以縮成 V-ing。

這是書面英文（報告、email）常見的壓縮法。口說少用，但看得懂很重要。

連接詞和重複的主詞拿掉，動詞改 -ing。意思由上下文決定（因為／當…時／然後）。
原本的兩句壓成一句
When we checked the samples, we found a crack.Checking the samples, we found a crack.
The line was stopped, and it was restarted at 3 p.m.Stopped for an hour, the line was restarted at 3 p.m.（被動 → -ed 開頭）
We will ship on Friday, and that will give you two weeks.We will ship on Friday, giving you two weeks.（放後面補充結果）

唯一要守的規則：兩句主詞要同一個人

Checking the samples, a crack was found. ← 裂縫自己去檢查樣品？

Checking the samples, we found a crack.

POINT
  • 兩句主詞相同 → 去連接詞、去主詞、動詞改 -ing（被動改 -ed）。
  • 讀的時候看到句首 V-ing，先找逗點後面的主詞，那就是做動作的人。
  • 自己寫：報告可以用，口說先不用。
3-6

受詞補語：讓誰做什麼、看到誰在做什麼

第 2 章的句型 ⑤，C 的位置換成動作。

S + V + O + 動作：我們「要」客戶「確認」；我「看到」他「離開」。O 後面那個動作穿哪套衣服，看前面的動詞。

We want the customer to approve the drawing.
動詞O 後面穿什麼
want / ask / expect / need / tell / allow
要、請、期待、告訴、允許		to V：We asked the supplier to resend the invoice.
They expect us to deliver by June.
make / let / have
使、讓、叫		原形（不加 to）：The manager made us redo the test.
Let me check.　I'll have the team look into it.
helpto 可加可不加：Please help me (to) find the file.
see / hear / watch / notice
感官		原形 = 看到整件事；-ing = 看到進行中
I saw him leave.　I saw him talking to the customer.
have / get + 物-ed = 把東西弄成「被做好」：We had the samples tested.
I'll get the drawing updated today.

你最常錯的兩個

Let me to check. → Let me check.

The customer made us to change the spec. → made us change

POINT
  • want / ask / expect / tell + 人 + to V。
  • make / let / have + 人 + 原形，不加 to。
  • have / get + 物 + V-ed = 把東西弄好（請人做）。
