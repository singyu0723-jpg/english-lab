這一章跟你的關係：你問過「libra is moving today iam not sure if he is attend meeting 這句對嗎」。問句和否定句的毛病多半在一個地方：誰來扛「問」和「不」。英文規定一定要有一個助動詞站出來扛，沒有就派 do 上場。這一章就講這件事。
5-1

四種句子：直述、疑問、命令、感嘆

同一個畫面，四種說法。

命令句：動詞原形開頭，職場加 please 或改成問句

Send me the file.（太直接）→ Please send me the file. → Could you send me the file?

Let's move on.（提議大家一起）

Don't forget to cc the manager.（否定命令）

對客戶和主管：用 Could you… / Would you mind…；對自己團隊：Please + 原形就夠。

感嘆句：知道就好

What a great result!

How fast!

職場口語比較常直接說：That's great. / That was fast.

POINT
  • 命令句 = 原形開頭；職場加 please，對客戶改成 Could you…?
  • Let's = 大家一起；Don't + 原形 = 不要。
5-2

疑問句的原理：找一個助動詞搬到前面

問句不是「加問號」，是把助動詞搬到主詞前面。

句子裡本來就有 be、will、can、have 的，直接把它搬到最前面。什麼都沒有的，派 do / does / did 出來，動詞回原形。

直述句問句（搬誰）
The sample is ready.Is the sample ready?
You have sent it.Have you sent it?
They will approve it.Will they approve it?
He attends the meeting.Does he attend the meeting?（s 給 does 扛）
You received it.Did you receive it?

Wh- 問句：疑問詞放最前面，後面照上面的規則

When will they approve it?

What did you receive?

Why is the sample late?

How many units do you need?

例外：問「誰做的」，who 自己就是主詞，不用搬：Who sent this?

你實際犯過的

I'm not sure if he is attend the meeting.

I'm not sure if he will attend the meeting. / if he's attending the meeting.

is 和 attend 兩個動詞打架。要嘛 is attending（進行式），要嘛 will attend。這種「if 後面的問句」第 6 章再講。

POINT
  • 有 be / will / can / have → 搬到主詞前。
  • 什麼都沒有 → 派 do / does / did，動詞回原形。
  • Wh- 字放最前面，後面照搬。Who 當主詞不用搬。
5-3

否定句的原理：not 貼在助動詞後面

not 要有東西可以貼，沒有就派 do。

有助動詞：not 貼上去

We cannot / can't ship yet.

It isn't ready.

I haven't received it.

沒助動詞：派 do 來貼

We don't have it in stock.

He doesn't work here.

I didn't receive it.

三個常錯

I not receive it. → I didn't receive it.

He doesn't works here. → He doesn't work here.

s 已經給 does 扛了

We have no received it. → We haven't received it.

no 和 not 的差別

not 貼動詞：We do not have stock.

no 貼名詞：We have no stock.

There is no problem. = There isn't any problem.

委婉的否定

I'm afraid we can't make it by Friday.

Unfortunately, the part is not available.

I don't think that will work.

英文習慣把「我不認為」放前面：I don't think it will work，不說 I think it won't work。

POINT
  • not 貼在助動詞後面；沒助動詞派 do / does / did，動詞回原形。
  • not 貼動詞，no 貼名詞。
  • 否定意見放前面：I don't think…
5-4

總整理：一張表看完

句子裡有問句否定句
be（is / are / was）Is it ready?It isn't ready.
will / can / shouldCan you ship it?We can't ship it.
have + V-edHave you sent it?I haven't sent it.
只有一般動詞（現在）Do you need it? / Does he need it?I don't need it. / He doesn't need it.
只有一般動詞（過去）Did you receive it?I didn't receive it.

附加問句：確認一下的語氣

You sent it, didn't you?

The sample is ready, isn't it?

They can't make it, can they?

前面肯定、後面否定；前面否定、後面肯定。口說常用，email 少用。

回答 Yes / No 問題：跟著助動詞答

Did you send it? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

Is it ready? — Yes, it is. / Not yet.

Can you ship by Friday? — Yes, we can. / I'm afraid we can't.

POINT
  • 問句和否定句用同一個助動詞；回答也用同一個。
  • 附加問句：前肯後否、前否後肯。
