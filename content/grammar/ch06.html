這一章跟你的關係：「我不確定他會不會來」「請告訴我什麼時候出貨」「客戶說他們需要折扣」——這三種句子都是把一句話塞進另一句話裡。塞進去的那句，形狀要變。這一章講怎麼變。
6-1

間接疑問句：問句塞進句子裡，就不再倒裝

塞進去的問句，變回「主詞 + 動詞」的順序。

直接問「When will they ship?」要搬助動詞。但放進「I don't know…」後面，它變成一個名詞（一件不知道的事），不再是問句，所以不搬、不加 do、不加問號。

直接問（倒裝）塞進句子（不倒裝）
Where is the sample?Do you know where the sample is?
What did he say?I'm not sure what he said.（did 消失，動詞回過去式）
How much does it cost?Please tell me how much it costs.
Why is it late?Could you explain why it is late?

Yes / No 問題塞進去：用 if 或 whether

Is he coming? → I'm not sure if he's coming.
Can we ship early? → Let me check whether we can ship early.

if 和 whether 這裡都是「是否」。口說多用 if；正式或後面接 or not 用 whether：whether or not we can ship。

職場最常用的開頭

Do you know …?　Could you tell me …?　I'm not sure ….　Let me check ….　I wonder ….　It depends on ….

Could you tell me when the parts will arrive?　It depends on how many units they order.

POINT
  • 問句塞進句子 → 不倒裝、不用 do、主詞在動詞前。
  • Yes/No 問題塞進去用 if / whether。
  • Could you tell me + 問句 是最禮貌又最安全的問法。
6-2

that / if / whether 名詞子句：一整句當一個名詞

that + 一句話 = 一個「事情」。

第 2 章說受詞可以是一整句話。that 就是把整句話包起來的括號，包好之後可以放在主詞、受詞、補語的位置。口說裡 that 常省略。

位置例句
受詞（最常用）We confirmed (that) the samples passed.
I think (that) we can make it.　The customer said (that) they need a discount.
形容詞後面I'm sure (that) it will work.　We're glad (that) you're satisfied.
I'm afraid (that) we can't.
主詞（正式，用 It 開頭）It is clear that the cost is too high.
It seems that the supplier missed the date.
補語（= 後面）The problem is that we have no stock.
The good news is that the test passed.

that 和 if / whether 怎麼選

that：說一件確定的事

I know that he's coming.

我知道他會來（確定）

if / whether：說一件不確定「是否」的事

I don't know if he's coming.

我不知道他是否會來

you said that… 的時態要跟著往後退

The customer said (that) they need a discount.（口語常保持現在式，意思沒變）
The customer said (that) they needed a discount.（正式寫法往後退一格）

6-3 細講。

POINT
  • that 是括號，把一句話包成一個名詞；口說可省略。
  • 確定的事用 that，是否的事用 if / whether。
  • The problem is that… / It seems that… 是報告常用開頭。
6-3

引述句：轉述別人的話

直接引述抄原話；間接引述把人稱和時間移到你的位置。

email 裡轉述客戶、主管的話天天在用。直接引述加引號照抄；間接引述把「我／你」換成「他／他們」，時態往後退一格（因為說話是過去的事）。

原話（直接）轉述（間接）
"I am busy."He said he was busy.
"We will ship on Monday."They said they would ship on Monday.
"We have sent the PO."They said they had sent the PO.
"Can you reduce the price?"He asked if we could reduce the price.（問句 → if，不倒裝）
"When will it arrive?"She asked when it would arrive.
"Please send the drawing."He asked us to send the drawing.（命令 → ask + 人 + to V）

退一格的對照

原話轉述原話轉述
am / is / arewas / werewillwould
do / does（現在式）did（過去式）cancould
did / have donehad donemusthad to

時間和地點的字也要移

today → that day　tomorrow → the next day　yesterday → the day before　here → there　this → that

"We'll call you tomorrow." → They said they would call us the next day.

email 裡轉述通常直接寫日期最清楚：They said they would call on Oct 14.

轉述用的動詞

say（說）He said that…　tell + 人（告訴誰）He told me that…　ask（問／請求）She asked if… / asked us to…
mention（提到）　confirm（確認）　explain（解釋）　suggest（建議）

He said me that… → He told me that… / He said that…

POINT
  • 轉述：人稱移到你的位置、時態退一格（will → would、can → could、現在 → 過去）。
  • 問句轉述用 if / wh-，不倒裝；命令轉述用 ask + 人 + to V。
  • say that… / tell 人 that…；沒有 say 人。
