這一章是選修：倒裝句你自己幾乎不會寫，但多益閱讀和客戶的正式 email 會出現。目標是看到認得。後半的插入語、同位語則是你寫 email 可以馬上用的。
7-1

倒裝的原理：把重點搬到最前面，助動詞跟著往前

倒裝 = 問句的搬法，但目的不是問，是強調。

第 5 章問句把助動詞搬到主詞前。倒裝句是：某個字被搬到句首強調（通常是否定字或 only），句子以為自己是問句，助動詞就跟著往前。

POINT
  • 否定字或 only 搬到句首 → 後面照問句的方式倒裝。
  • 讀的時候：看到句首是 Never / Not only / Only… 而且後面像問句，就是倒裝，意思不變，只是在強調。
7-2

倒裝的類型：看到認得就好

類型例句還原成正常句
否定字開頭
Never / Rarely / Not only…		Never have we had such a serious delay.
Not only did the price go up, but the lead time also got longer.		We have never had…
The price not only went up…
Only 開頭Only then did we realize the spec was wrong.
Only after testing can we confirm the result.		We realized only then…
So / Neither 的接話
口說天天用		A: I'm busy this week. B: So am I.
A: We can't make it. B: Neither can we.		I am busy too.
We can't either.
地點／方向開頭Attached is the revised quotation.
Here comes the customer.
Enclosed are the test reports.		The revised quotation is attached.
假設語氣省 if
第 10 章再講		Should you have any questions, please let me know.If you have any questions…

你 email 真的會用到的三個

Attached is the updated drawing.　Attached are the two reports.（動詞跟後面的名詞一致）

Should you have any questions, please feel free to contact me.

So do we. / Neither do we.（我們也是／我們也不）

POINT
  • 自己只需要會三個：Attached is…、Should you have…、So / Neither + 助動詞 + 主詞。
  • 其他倒裝遇到時還原成正常句讀，意思一樣。
7-3

插入語：用兩個逗點塞一句補充

句子中間插一段，前後各一個逗點，拿掉不影響句子。

常用插入語例句
however / therefore / for exampleThe sample, however, failed the pull test.
We can, for example, change the jacket material.
I think / I believe / as far as I knowThe parts, I believe, will arrive on Friday.
if necessary / if possible / of courseWe can, if necessary, run a second test.
in other words / that isThe MOQ is 5,000, in other words, one full reel.

插入語放句首也可以：However, the sample failed. 放中間比較像書面英文。

7-4

同位語：名詞後面再補一個名詞解釋它

A, B, … → B 是用來解釋 A 的。

例句用途
Our customer, a US networking company, requires UL certification.介紹對方是誰
The product, a 28 AWG twinax cable, is designed for 56G.解釋產品是什麼
I'm writing about PO 4471, the order placed last week.指明是哪一個
Erick, senior engineer at FIT, will be your contact.介紹自己

同位語比關係子句短（第 11 章會講 who / which），email 裡介紹人或東西，用同位語最乾淨。

POINT
  • 插入語：兩個逗點夾一段，拿掉不影響句子。
  • 同位語：名詞後面用逗點再接一個名詞解釋它，介紹人和產品最好用。
