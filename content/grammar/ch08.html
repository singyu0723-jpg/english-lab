這一章跟你的關係：你問過「為何是 was made」。製造業的英文滿地都是被動：樣品被測了、訂單被取消了、圖面被改了。因為重點是東西，不是誰做的。這一章把被動的三個條件講清楚，順便處理你寫過的 was happened。
8-1

被動的原理：把「被打到的東西」拉到句首

主動是人 → 東西；被動是東西 ← 人。

第 2 章句型 ③ S → O。把 O 拉到前面當主詞，動詞改成 be + V-ed，原本的 S 退到 by 後面（通常直接省略）。

三個條件，缺一不可

條件例
1動詞要能打到東西（有受詞）test / send / make / cancel / approve ✓happen / arrive / exist ✗（沒受詞，不能被動）
2be 要跟著時態和主詞變is tested / are tested / was tested / will be tested / has been tested
3動詞用過去分詞（第三型）was sent（不是 sended）was madewas chosen

你寫過的錯

The problem was happened yesterday. → The problem happened yesterday.

條件 1：happen 沒受詞

The samples was sent. → The samples were sent.

條件 2

The drawing was send yesterday. → was sent

條件 3

POINT
  • 被動 = 東西當主詞 + be + V-ed；做的人用 by，不重要就省。
  • 三個條件：動詞有受詞、be 跟時態、動詞用第三型。
  • happen / arrive / occur 沒有被動。
8-2

各時態的被動：只有 be 在變

V-ed 不動，動的是前面的 be。

時態be 的樣子例句
現在簡單is / are + V-edAll samples are tested before shipping.
過去簡單was / were + V-edThe order was cancelled last week.
未來will be + V-edThe drawing will be updated tomorrow.
現在完成has / have been + V-edThe PO has been approved.
現在進行is / are being + V-edThe samples are being tested right now.
助動詞can / must / should be + V-edThis must be confirmed by the customer.It can't be changed.

email 裡最常出現的六句

The samples were shipped on Oct 8.

Your order has been received.

The meeting has been rescheduled to Friday.

The price is based on 5,000 pcs.

The drawing is attached.

This part is made in Vietnam.

POINT
  • 被動句的時態全部長在 be 上：is / was / will be / has been / is being。
  • has been + V-ed = 「已經被…了」，email 最常用。
8-3

句型 ④ ⑤ 的被動

句型 ④（人＋物）：兩個受詞，誰都可以當主詞

They gave us a discount.

→ We were given a discount.（人當主詞，最自然）

→ A discount was given to us.（物當主詞，人要加 to）

The customer sent us the PO. → We were sent the PO. / The PO was sent to us.

句型 ⑤（O = C）：受詞當主詞，C 留在原地

We keep the samples dry. → The samples are kept dry.

They call this part the housing. → This part is called the housing.

The customer found the connector damaged. → The connector was found damaged.

O + 動作的被動：原形要補回 to

They made us redo the test. → We were made to redo the test.

They asked us to resend it. → We were asked to resend it.

I saw him leave. → He was seen to leave.

主動時 make / see 後面不加 to，變被動就要補 to。

POINT
  • 句型 ④ 被動：人當主詞最自然（We were given…）。
  • 句型 ⑤ 被動：C 不動（is kept dry、is called…）。
  • be made to V、be asked to V：被動要補 to。
8-4

各式被動句型：by 以外的介係詞、不用被動的時候

不是 by 的被動

句型例句
be interested inWe are interested in your new series.
be made of / fromThe jacket is made of PVC.（看得出材料）Paper is made from wood.（看不出原料）
be based onThe quote is based on current copper prices.
be covered with / be filled withThe connector was covered with dust.
be known for / be satisfied withThey are satisfied with the result.
be scheduled for / be due onThe audit is scheduled for Nov 3.

get + V-ed：口語的被動，強調「發生了變化」

The sample got damaged during shipping.

The order got cancelled.

I got promoted.

什麼時候用主動就好

被動是為了把東西放前面。如果你本來就在講人（我們做了什麼），就用主動，不要為了「看起來正式」硬改被動。

We will ship the samples on Friday.（告訴客戶我們會做 → 主動，有承諾感）

The samples will be shipped on Friday.（通知一個事實 → 被動也可以）

The quotation was sent by me yesterday. → I sent the quotation yesterday.

做的人就是你，直接說

It is said that… / be supposed to

It is said that the supplier is closing the factory.（據說）

The parts are supposed to arrive today.（應該要到了，但…）

It is expected that prices will go up next quarter.

POINT
  • 記固定搭配：interested in、based on、made of、satisfied with、scheduled for。
  • get + V-ed 是口語被動，強調變化。
  • 做的人是你，就用主動；不要硬寫 by me。
