這一章跟你的關係：助動詞是「語氣的旋鈕」。同一件事，must / should / could / might 轉出來的強度不一樣。你跟客戶講話常常太直接（You must send…），這一章把旋鈕刻度畫給你。
9-1

助動詞是什麼：動詞前面的語氣旋鈕

助動詞 + 原形。它不改變動作，改變「多確定、多強硬」。

三條共同規則

① 後面接原形：He can goes → He can go.
② 不加 s、不加 to：She musts / must to → She must check.
③ 問句搬它、否定貼它：Can you…? / We cannot…（第 5 章）

POINT
  • 助動詞 + 原形，不加 s、不加 to。
  • 它是語氣旋鈕：might → may → could → should → will → must。
9-2

must / have to：一定要

must：說話者自己覺得一定要

We must finish the test today.
You must wear the ESD strap.

自己的要求、規定

have to：外在條件逼的

We have to ship by Friday — the customer's line stops otherwise.
I had to redo the report.（過去只能用 had to）

客戶、規則、情況逼的；must 沒有過去式

否定完全不同：must not = 禁止，don't have to = 不必

You must not touch the pins.（禁止）
You don't have to send the original.（不必，寄掃描檔就好）

You must not send the original.（這會變成「禁止寄」）

must 的另一個意思：一定是（推測）

The line is down. There must be a power issue.（一定是）
He didn't reply. He must be on leave.

對客戶不要用 must

You must send the PO by Friday.

We would need the PO by Friday to hold the schedule. / Could you send the PO by Friday?

POINT
  • must = 我覺得一定要；have to = 情況逼的；過去式只有 had to。
  • must not = 禁止；don't have to = 不必。
  • must be = 一定是（推測）。對客戶少用 must。
9-3

should / shall：應該

should = 建議、合理的期待。比 must 軟一格。

We should confirm the quantity before we quote.（建議）
The parts should arrive by Wednesday.（應該會到，合理預期）
You shouldn't use that supplier for high-speed cable.

should 的問句：Should we…? = 我們要不要…

Should we wait for the test results?　Should I cc the manager?

shall：現在只剩兩個用法

Shall we start?（提議，= Let's start?）　Shall I send you the file?（主動提供協助）

合約裡的 shall = must（The Seller shall deliver…），看得懂就好。

ought to = should，比較少用

We ought to double-check the drawing.

POINT
  • should = 應該（建議／合理預期）；Should we…? = 要不要…
  • Shall we…? 提議；Shall I…? 我來幫你…?
9-4

will / would：會、願意、客氣

用法例句
will 未來、承諾I will send it today.　It will take two weeks.
will 願意／請求Will you join the call?　The machine won't start.（不肯動）
would 客氣的 willWould you send me the file?（比 Will you 軟）
I would suggest a thicker jacket.（我會建議…，比 I suggest 軟）
would like = want 的禮貌版I would like to confirm the schedule.　Would you like a sample?
would 過去的 will（轉述）They said they would ship on Monday.（第 6 章）
would 假設（第 10 章）If the price were lower, we would order more.

email 三句萬用

I would like to… / I'd like to…（我想要…）
Would you mind…?（可否…？）
I would appreciate it if you could…（如果你能…，我會很感激）

POINT
  • will = 會／承諾；would = 客氣版、過去版、假設版。
  • I'd like to… 比 I want to… 專業；Would you… 比 Will you… 客氣。
9-5

can / could：能力、許可、可能、請求

用法例句
can 能力We can produce 10,000 meters a day.　This connector can support 10 A.
can 許可You can use the lab after 5.　Can I take a photo?
can't 不可能（推測）It can't be the cable — we tested it twice.
could 過去的能力Last year we could get copper at a better price.
could 客氣的請求Could you send me the spec?（比 Can you 客氣）
could 可能性／建議We could try a thinner wire.（可以考慮…）
The delay could be due to customs.

be able to：can 的替身，用在 can 不能出現的地方

We will be able to ship next week.（will 後面不能接 can）
We haven't been able to find the cause.（完成式）

POINT
  • can = 能／可以；could = 過去的 can、客氣的請求、「可以考慮」。
  • will be able to、have been able to：can 進不去的地方用 be able to。
9-6

may / might：可能、許可

The shipment may be delayed by the typhoon.（可能，50%）
It might work, but I'm not sure.（也許，比 may 更不確定）
May I ask who approved this?（正式的許可）

You may want to check the latest drawing.　← 很客氣的「建議你看一下」

推測的刻度

多確定說法例句
一定是must beIt must be a wiring issue.
應該是should beThe parts should be there by now.
可能是may / might / could beIt might be a software bug.
不可能是can't beIt can't be the cable.
POINT
  • may / might = 可能；You may want to… 是最客氣的建議。
  • 推測：must be → should be → might be → can't be。
9-7

助動詞 + have + V-ed：回頭看過去

把旋鈕轉向過去：對已經發生的事做推測或評論。

句型意思例句
must have + V-ed一定是（已經）…了They must have missed the email.
should have + V-ed早該…（但沒有）→ 檢討We should have tested it before shipping.
could have + V-ed本來可以…（但沒有）We could have shipped earlier if the PO had come in.
may / might have + V-ed可能已經…了The package might have been delayed at customs.
can't have + V-ed不可能已經…They can't have received it yet — it only shipped today.

should have + V-ed 是檢討報告的核心句型：We should have…（我們早該…）。

POINT
  • 助動詞 + have + V-ed = 對過去的推測或評論。
  • should have = 早該；must have = 一定是已經；could have = 本來可以。
