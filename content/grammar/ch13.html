13-1

介係詞是怎麼來的：位置 → 時間 → 抽象

每個介係詞先是一張空間圖，再借去講時間，最後借去講抽象的關係。

第 1 章六個的時間版，再看一次

字圖時間
at一個點at 10 a.m.　at noon　at the end of the month　at the moment
on貼在一天上on Friday　on Oct 14　on the delivery date
in一段範圍裡in May　in 2026　in the morning　in two weeks（兩週後）
by不超過那條線by Friday（最晚週五）
for一段長度for two weeks（持續兩週）　valid for 30 days
to到達終點from Monday to Friday
POINT
  • 每個介係詞：空間圖 → 時間 → 抽象，同一張圖。
  • at 點、on 日、in 段、by 期限、for 長度。

13-3

總複習：最常混的幾組

in two weeks vs within two weeks

in two weeks = 兩週後（那個時候）
within two weeks = 兩週內（任何時候）

We'll ship in two weeks.　Please reply within two weeks.

by vs until

by = 最晚到那時（一次性）
until = 持續到那時

Reply by Friday.　The offer is valid until Friday.

made of vs made from

of：看得出原材料（a cable made of copper）
from：已經變了樣（paper made from wood）

on time vs in time

on time = 準時（貼在那個時間點上）
in time = 來得及（在範圍內趕上）

The shipment arrived on time.　We got the PO in time to start Monday.

at the end vs in the end

at the end of + 時間 = 在…的尾端
in the end = 最後（結果）

at the end of the month　In the end, we chose supplier B.

talk to vs talk with vs talk about

to 對誰講　with 跟誰討論　about 講什麼

I talked to the customer about the delay.
