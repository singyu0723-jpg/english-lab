13-1

介係詞是怎麼來的：位置 → 時間 → 抽象

每個介係詞先是一張空間圖，再借去講時間，最後借去講抽象的關係。

  • 每個介係詞：空間圖 → 時間 → 抽象，同一張圖。
  • at 點、on 日、in 段、by 期限、for 長度。

13-3

總複習：最常混的幾組

in two weeks vs within two weeks

in two weeks = 兩週後（那個時候）

within two weeks = 兩週內（任何時候）

We'll ship in two weeks.

Please reply within two weeks.

by vs until

by = 最晚到那時（一次性）

until = 持續到那時

Reply by Friday.

The offer is valid until Friday.

made of vs made from

of：看得出原材料（a cable made of copper）

from：已經變了樣（paper made from wood）

on time vs in time

on time = 準時（貼在那個時間點上）

in time = 來得及（在範圍內趕上）

The shipment arrived on time.

We got the PO in time to start Monday.

at the end vs in the end

at the end of + 時間 = 在…的尾端

in the end = 最後（結果）

at the end of the month

In the end, we chose supplier B.

talk to vs talk with vs talk about

to 對誰講

with 跟誰討論

about 講什麼

I talked to the customer about the delay.
