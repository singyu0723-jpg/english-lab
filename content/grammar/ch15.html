這一章跟你的關係：你問過 as X as Y 要細講。報價、比方案、講規格，全是比較：比較便宜、一樣快、最可靠。這一章三種比較一次講完，再把你常用的「比…多 20%」「越…越…」補上。
15-1

三種比較，三張圖

形容詞怎麼變形

長度比較級最高級

-y 結尾easierearlierheavierthe easiestthe earliest
長（2 音節以上）more reliablemore expensivemore flexiblethe most reliablethe most expensive
不規則good → betterbad → worsemany/much → morelittle → lessfar → furtherthe bestthe worstthe mostthe leastthe furthest

more cheaper

more better

the most fastest

一次只能用一種變形

POINT
  • as…as 一樣；-er than / more…than 比；the -est / the most 最。
  • 短字加 -er / -est，長字加 more / most，不能兩個一起用。
  • good–better–best、bad–worse–worst、much–more–most。
15-2

原級：as … as

as 形容詞 as 對象。兩個 as 中間放形容詞或副詞的原形。

The new supplier is as reliable as the old one.

We can ship as early as next Monday.（最早下週一）

Please reply as soon as possible.（ASAP）

否定：not as … as = 不如

Supplier B is not as fast as A.（B 不如 A 快 = A 比較快）

This batch is not as good as the last one.

倍數放在 as 前面

The new line is twice as fast as the old one.（兩倍快）

Copper is three times as expensive as aluminum.

as … as 的三個固定用法（你問過的）

句型意思
as soon as possible / you can盡快
as much as possibleas many as 50 units盡量多／多達 50 個（much 不可數、many 可數）
as far as I know / I'm concerned就我所知／就我而言（第 7 章的插入語）
as long as只要（第 10 章）
as well as以及（= and，正式）：the drawing as well as the spec

「什麼時候只有後面的 as」：as 單獨出現是連接詞或介係詞（as a result、as you know、work as an engineer），跟比較無關。兩個 as 才是比較。

POINT
  • as 原形 as；not as…as = 不如；twice as…as = 兩倍。
  • as soon as / as much as / as far as / as long as / as well as 五組固定用法。
15-3

比較級：-er than / more … than

Supplier A is cheaper than B.

The new design is more reliable than the old one.

Air freight is faster but more expensive than sea.

比多少：數字放前面

A is 20% cheaper than B.

The new cable is 2 mm thinner.

It takes two days longer.

程度副詞

much / far / a lot cheaper（便宜很多）

slightly / a little cheaper（便宜一點）

even cheaper（更便宜）

very cheaper → much cheaper

very 不能修飾比較級

越…越…

The more you order, the lower the unit price.（訂越多，單價越低）

The earlier we get the PO, the earlier we can ship.

Prices are getting higher and higher.（越來越高）

than 後面放什麼

A is cheaper than B.

This is better than I expected.

It costs more than we budgeted.

than 後面接人用受格：faster than me（口語）/ than I am（正式）

than 和 then

cheaper then → cheaper than

then 是「然後」

POINT
  • 比多少放前面：20% cheaper、two days longer。
  • much / far / slightly 修飾比較級，不用 very。
  • The more…, the lower…：談數量折扣最好用。
15-4

最高級：the -est / the most

This is the cheapest option.

Supplier C has the shortest lead time.

Reliability is the most important factor for this customer.

範圍怎麼說

the best in the industry（在…之中：地方、群體）

the cheapest of the three（在…之中：數量）

the largest order we have ever had（我們有史以來）

one of the best suppliers（最…之一，後面複數）

最高級常跟現在完成式

This is the largest order we have ever received.

It's the worst delay I have seen this year.

軟一點的最高級

This is by far the best option.（遠遠最好）

It's one of the most reliable connectors on the market.（之一，留餘地）

This is the second-largest customer.（第二大）

三種比較的對照

原級比較級最高級
A is as cheap as B.A is cheaper than B.A is the cheapest of the three.
A is not as reliable as B.B is more reliable than A.B is the most reliable.
as good asbetter thanthe best
POINT
  • 最高級一定有 the；範圍用 in（群體）或 of（數量）。
  • the largest order we have ever had：最高級 + 現在完成。
  • one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞：留餘地的說法。
